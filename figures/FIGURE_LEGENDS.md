# Main manuscript figure legends

**Figure 1. Architecture-dependent effects of LoRA adaptation on held-out classification performance.** (A) Matthews correlation coefficient (MCC) for the frozen and LoRA-adapted M0 (MLP), M1 (CNN), and M3 (BiLSTM–Transformer) checkpoints on the independent Test partition. Connected circle and square markers identify each architecture's frozen and adapted results. (B) Corresponding Group-OOD results; these comparisons are exploratory. (C, D) Paired LoRA-minus-frozen MCC differences on Test and Group-OOD, respectively, with the archived 95% component-bootstrap confidence intervals. The dashed line denotes no difference. Intervals that meet or cross zero do not support a directional claim. No bootstrap was rerun for this figure.

**Figure 2. Heterogeneity of positive-class recovery across biologically withheld groups.** (A) Positive-class recall for each of 24 verified Group-OOD biological blocks (B01–B24) and six selected checkpoints. The adjacent column gives each block's positive-protein count; B01–B24 correspond to B001–B024 in Supplementary Table S3 and to OOD_BLOCK_001–OOD_BLOCK_024 in the original block records. Exact component mappings are provided in Figure_2_heatmap_data.csv. Fr denotes frozen, and LoRA denotes adapted. Colour spans recall 0–1 without interpolation. (B) Overall positive-protein recall (321 proteins) and unweighted mean of the 24 block recalls for each checkpoint. These are distinct estimands. Frozen M3 recovered 168 of 170 positive proteins in B01, while 18 of 24 blocks had zero recall. Group-OOD analysis is exploratory.

**Figure 3. ESKAPE proteome predictions and functional-annotation evidence.** (A) Fraction of inference-eligible proteins called model-positive in six reference proteomes by six selected checkpoints, calculated from total model-positive counts divided by eligible proteins. (B) Flagged/eligible counts and fractions for eligible core AMRFinderPlus annotations; denominators vary by strain and are printed in every cell. These values describe annotation concordance, not experimentally measured sensitivity or proteome-wide accuracy. Fr denotes frozen. (C) Mutually exclusive Swiss-Prot and mapped InterPro evidence categories in the fixed, audited 552-protein cohort: both (343), mapped InterPro only (164), Swiss-Prot only (3), and neither source (42). The 52 exact full-length reviewed-sequence matches are a subset, not a fifth category. Model-positive predictions and computational sequence/domain evidence do not establish resistance activity or protein novelty.
